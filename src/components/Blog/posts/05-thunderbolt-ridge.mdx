  Wow! Amazing ridge scrambling, and the approach sure warms you up. Also pleasantly surprised that most of the rock doesn't budge much with awesome texture and movement. I found the ridge to get more technical and exposed the further you get, until the final 100-200 ft. of 3rd class. Interestingly, there are some airplane parts scattered around 100 yards south of the ridge's base (maybe this link explains some? zettasaprons.blogspot.com/p…)

The FA must have been quite committing - as a scramble, it's just barely there in places. Kind of magical. But I agree on the crux grading in the MP description - definitely a step up from the other classic scrambles of the Wasatch (Pfeiff North Ridge, Geurt's Ridge, West Slabs, South Ridge of Superior).

Some beta/spray:
Approached via red pine -> going around the south side of Pfeiff + Upwop -> dropping into Hogum via the gulley at 40.52914034635002, -111.71603054251484
